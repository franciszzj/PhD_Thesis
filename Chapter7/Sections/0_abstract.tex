Building on Chapter~6, this chapter studies the video counterpart of reference-conditioned generation.
Reference-to-video (R2V) generation must preserve subject identity from reference images while also maintaining temporal coherence, but current methods usually rely on expensive reference image-video-text triplets that are difficult to scale.
This chapter introduces Saber, a scalable zero-shot framework that removes the need for explicit R2V data.
Trained exclusively on video-text pairs, Saber uses a masked training strategy and a tailored attention-based design to learn identity-consistent and reference-aware representations.
Mask augmentation is further integrated to mitigate the copy-paste artifacts common in reference-to-video generation.
Experiments on OpenS2V-Eval show that Saber generalizes across different numbers of references and outperforms methods trained with explicit R2V data.
Within the thesis, this chapter completes the controllable-generation line by extending reference preservation from images to videos.
