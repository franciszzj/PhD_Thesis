\noindent \textbf{Statement of individual contribution.}
This work was developed during a second internship at Meta, and the paper has seventeen authors. As first author, I proposed the central idea of this chapter: that reference-to-video generation can be learned from ordinary video--text pairs by building synthetic references from randomly sampled, partially masked frames. I designed the masked training strategy, the attention-mask formulation, and the spatial mask augmentation, implemented the method, designed and ran all experiments and ablations reported here, and wrote the manuscript. My mentors, who are listed last among the authors, provided research guidance. The remaining co-authors provided technical support for training infrastructure and large-scale data pipelines, baseline reproduction, and evaluation. Section~\ref{sec:individual_contributions} gives the corresponding statement for every chapter of this thesis.
