\vspace{1.5cm}
\begin{minipage}{0.8\textwidth}
\centering
The next three chapters study control over what a system reports about a scene, in the sense of Section~\ref{sec:unifying_argument}. Using panoptic scene graph generation (PSG), they treat relation prediction as the interface through which a scene can be controlled, and progressively widen it: control over the head--tail trade-off in Chapter~3, control through language priors in Chapter~4, and control over the relation vocabulary itself in Chapter~5.
