{\Huge\bfseries Part II: Control in Visual Generation\par}
\vspace{1.5cm}
\begin{minipage}{0.8\textwidth}
\centering
The following two chapters study control over what a system generates, in the sense of Section~\ref{sec:unifying_argument}. Using reference-conditioned image and video synthesis, they show that a reference supplied to a generator is not yet a constraint on it. The intended behavior must be supervised explicitly: spatially in Chapter~6, and over time in Chapter~7.
