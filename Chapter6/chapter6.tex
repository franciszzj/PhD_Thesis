\noindent \textbf{Statement of individual contribution.}
This work was developed during an internship at Meta, and the paper has fourteen authors. As first author, I proposed the central idea of this chapter: reading the cross-attention map between target queries and reference keys as a soft correspondence field, and regularizing it during training. I formulated the \leffa{} loss, implemented the method, designed and ran all experiments and ablations reported here, and wrote the manuscript. My mentors, who are listed last among the authors, provided research guidance. The remaining co-authors provided technical support, including engineering and infrastructure support for large-scale training, help with the human study, and comments on the manuscript. Section~\ref{sec:individual_contributions} gives the corresponding statement for every chapter of this thesis.
